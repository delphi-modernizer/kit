## Scale

| Probability or impact | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Low | Unlikely, or a contained inconvenience |
| Medium | Plausible, or a delay or cost the plan can absorb |
| High | Likely, or a threat to a milestone, data or the business |

Severity combines them: Low × Low is Low, High × High is Critical, and the owner states the reasoning for anything
in between.
